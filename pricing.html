Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our subscription plans.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. We'll prorate any differences fairly.

What happens if I go over my conversation limit?

We won't cut you off. If you exceed your monthly limit, we'll notify you and apply a simple overage rate. You can also upgrade your plan at any time to get a better per-conversation rate.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees, ever. We include onboarding and knowledge base training in your first month. For Enterprise plans, custom integration work may be quoted separately.

How does the free pilot work?

Our pilot program lets you try Nexify AI with real customer conversations for a limited time, often at a significant discount or even free. It's the best way to see the impact before committing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees. Your service continues until the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), SEPA direct debit, and bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.

When to Choose PAYG

Usage-based pricing works best for businesses with variable demand.

Seasonal Businesses

Tourism, hospitality, retail — businesses that have busy seasons and quiet periods. Pay more when you’re busy, less when you’re not.

Testing Phase

Just starting with AI customer service? Start with PAYG to prove the ROI before committing to a monthly plan.

Fluctuating Demand

Marketing campaigns, product launches, or unpredictable inquiry volume. PAYG flexes with your business needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about usage-based pricing.

What counts as a “conversation”?

A conversation is a single customer interaction session — from first message to resolution. If the same customer comes back the next day with a new question, that’s a new conversation.

Can I switch between PAYG and subscription?

Yes, you can switch billing models at any time. Just let us know and we’ll adjust your plan at the start of the next billing cycle.

Is there a minimum charge?

No minimums, no base fees. If you have zero conversations in a month, you pay zero. We do have a small one-time setup fee for new accounts.

How do volume discounts work?

Volume discounts are applied automatically based on your total monthly conversations. The more you use, the lower your per-conversation rate.

Can I set a monthly spending cap?

Absolutely. Set a maximum monthly budget and we’ll notify you when you’re approaching it. You decide what happens when the cap is reached.

What if I’m not sure which model to choose?

Start with our pilot program! You’ll get real data on your conversation volume, which makes it easy to choose the right pricing model afterwards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about custom solutions.

How long does a custom solution take?

It depends on complexity. Simple customizations (branding, basic integrations) can be done in 2-4 weeks. Complex projects with multiple integrations and custom model training typically take 6-12 weeks.

How much does a custom solution cost?

Custom solutions start at around €1,000/month plus a one-time setup fee. The exact cost depends on your requirements, integrations, and volume. Tell us about your needs and we’ll send a detailed quote.

Do you offer on-premise deployment?

Yes, for enterprise customers we support deployment on your own infrastructure or private cloud. This is ideal for organizations with strict data residency or security requirements.

Can I start with a standard plan and upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many customers start with a standard plan and then move to custom as their needs grow. We’ll work with you to design a solution that scales with your business.

What kind of integrations do you support?

Nearly anything with an API. Common integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, booking systems, ERP software, and internal tools. If your system has an API, we can connect to it.

Is there a pilot option for custom solutions?

Yes! We offer pilot programs for custom solutions too. You can test a scoped version of your custom setup before committing to a full rollout.
