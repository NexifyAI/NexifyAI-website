For technicians — about this demo
This page runs in live mode: the assistant answers from the real knowledge base (tenant configuration, domain verification and knowledge ingestion are complete). With ?mode=mock the widget runs on local scripted replies without a backend.
  • Click the bubble (bottom-right) to open the chat panel.
  • The AI disclosure bar is always visible at the top and cannot be closed.
  • On emergency keywords a fixed referral appears (112 / option 1 / out-of-hours post) — never an LLM answer.
  • The conversation keeps its session across turns (multi-turn works).
