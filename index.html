Questions, answered

How long does setup really take?

About three minutes of chatting. The AI builds your assistant as you describe your business; the email confirmation and embed snippet take two more clicks.

Do I need technical skills or a developer?

No. If you can paste one line of code into your website — or forward it to whoever manages your site — you're done. There is no backend integration.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You pick a plan: a fixed monthly subscription or pay-per-conversation. No credit card is required to start, and nothing is charged automatically.

What about privacy and AI rules?

Conversations are archived in the EU with automated PII redaction and fixed retention. The assistant always identifies itself as AI, and you confirm your own compliance responsibilities before going live.

Can it really speak my customers' languages?

Yes — 30+, including Dutch, French, German, Spanish and English. It detects the customer's language automatically and answers in kind.
