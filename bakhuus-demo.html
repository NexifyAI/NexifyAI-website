About this demo — how it works

This demo runs live: the answers come from the real knowledge base, built from your website, menu card and allergen table. You ask a question, the assistant searches that knowledge base and answers only with what is in it.

  • The bar at the top of the window always states that you are chatting with an AI assistant.
  • On order intent a direct button to your online ordering page appears (pickup only).
  • Questions about other businesses are not answered — your knowledge base stays yours.
  • The conversation keeps its session: you can ask several questions in a row.
